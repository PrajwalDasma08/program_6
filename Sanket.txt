Recursion in C

Defination of recursion 
>>>> recursion is the programming technique in which the fuction call it self directly or indirectly to solve the problem 
>>>> The idea is to divide A larger problem into smaller problems of the same time until a simple condiotion is reached .

Recursion Fuctions 

A recursion fuction is the fuction it call itself during its execution 
a recursion fuction must have a condition to stop a repeated function calls.

***Base case
> the base case is the condition to stop recursion .
> It represents the smallest or simplest version of the problem that can be solved directly .



***Recursion Case
> the recursion case should move the problem towards the base case .


***Types of recursion 

1 .Tail recursion
2 .Non Tail recursion

Tail recursion
<>>> Tail recursion occurs when the recursive function call is the last operation performed by the function .
 

Non Tail recursion 
>>> non tail recursion occurs when some operation still needs to be performed after the recursive call returns .
